## About the Service

Get My TG is a store for ready-to-use Telegram accounts. Buy, get the number, log in, use.

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose and Pay">
    Select an account from the catalog and place your order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get the Number">
    After payment, the bot will provide the phone number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log in via Telegram">
    Enter the number in the official client.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get the Code">
    Click "Get Code" on the website. If there's 2FA — the password is also there.
  </Step>
</Steps>
